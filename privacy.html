Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Dongqi Ads Automation is an internal advertising management tool operated by Dongqi Global Supplies. It is used for Meta advertising accounts and Facebook Pages that the operator has authorized. It is not a public customer relationship management service.

Information we access

With the permissions granted to the app, we may access advertising account and Page identifiers, campaign and ad settings, audience locations and targeting, budgets, ad text and images, delivery status, and performance reports supplied by Meta. We may also receive information that a person voluntarily sends to our business contact channel. The current advertising workflow does not automatically import WhatsApp chat contents, customer databases, or order records.

How we use information

We use this information to plan, create, review, monitor, and improve ads for our own authorized business account, and to respond to inquiries. Advertising performance figures may be saved in local project reports and notes. We do not sell the information obtained through this app.

Storage and access

Meta API credentials are stored locally on the operator's Windows computer, encrypted for the authorized Windows user with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Authorized local scripts retrieve them when needed to call Meta's official API. Advertising reports, plans, and manually supplied business feedback may be kept in local project files. No API credentials or customer records are intentionally published on this website.

Retention and deletion

We keep local records for as long as needed for advertising management, audit, and legitimate business recordkeeping. A request for access or deletion can be sent through the contact method below. We review and process requests manually and remove data under our control when appropriate. We cannot delete records controlled by Meta or another service; their own account and privacy controls apply. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Third parties

Meta provides the advertising platform, Page and WhatsApp-related services, and Marketing API. GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages. Those providers may process technical usage data under their own policies. This website does not add an analytics service or an advertising tracker.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Dongqi Global Supplies through WhatsApp at +86 135 1696 3929. Please identify the data or Meta account involved so we can assess the request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.
