Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Dongqi Ads Automation does not provide an automated public data deletion interface. Requests are handled manually.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Dongqi Global Supplies through WhatsApp at +86 135 1696 3929.
  2. Say that the request concerns “Dongqi Ads Automation data deletion.” Identify the relevant Meta account, Page, inquiry, or information you want us to remove. Do not include passwords or access tokens.
  3. We may ask for enough information to verify your authority over the data before taking action. We will review the request and reply through the same contact channel.

What we can remove

Where appropriate, we can manually remove locally held reports, notes, and contact information under our control, and revoke or delete locally stored app credentials. We will explain if a record must be retained for a legitimate reason. Data held by Meta or WhatsApp must also be managed through those services' own account and privacy controls; we cannot promise deletion from their systems.

Removing app access

An authorized Meta user can remove the app's permissions in their Meta account settings. The operator can also revoke the app's access token in Meta and remove the corresponding local encrypted credential. Revoking access stops future authorized API calls; it does not automatically erase historical records held by Meta.
